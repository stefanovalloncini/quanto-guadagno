# Impeccable Design Context — Quanto Guadagno

## Design Context

### Users
Italian workers — employees, freelancers (Partita IVA), and tax-curious citizens — who want to understand their real take-home pay. They arrive with a gross salary number and anxiety about how much goes to taxes. Their context is practical: paycheck in hand, career decision ahead, or simple curiosity about the Italian tax system. The tool must serve both first-time visitors quickly and returning users exploring scenarios.

### Brand Personality
**Editorial, warm, transparent.**

The interface channels the authority of Il Sole 24 Ore — data-rich, precise, typographically refined — while maintaining the clarity and accessibility ethos of Gov.uk. It is not cold or bureaucratic; warmth comes through the ivory backgrounds, serif display headings, and teal accents that feel human rather than institutional. Transparency is the core value: every number is explainable, every breakdown visible.

### Emotional Goals
The interface should evoke, in order of priority:
1. **Confidence & clarity** — users trust the results and understand their finances
2. **Relief & empowerment** — the intimidation of Italian tax complexity dissolves into understanding
3. **Calm & focus** — a serene, distraction-free environment for the numbers
4. **Curiosity & discovery** — scenarios, comparisons, and charts invite exploration

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Refined financial editorial — think quality newspaper data pages, not fintech dashboards
- **References:** Il Sole 24 Ore (authority, data density, editorial typography), Gov.uk (radical clarity, accessibility-first, no decoration without purpose)
- **Anti-references:** Generic SaaS gradients, gamified finance apps, dark-mode-only tech aesthetics, overly playful illustrations
- **Theme:** Light mode primary (warm ivory #fdfcfa), dark mode supported. The warmth of the palette (ivory, teal, burnt orange) distinguishes it from cold corporate blue
- **Typography:** Serif display headings (Instrument Serif) for editorial authority, sans-serif body (Satoshi) for modern readability, monospace (IBM Plex Mono) for numerical precision

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — Every element must earn its place. No ornamental gradients, no decorative illustrations without informational purpose. If a user can't understand what a number means at a glance, the design has failed.

2. **Data is the hero** — Numbers, breakdowns, and charts are the primary content. Typography, spacing, and color exist to make data scannable and comparable. Use the editorial tradition: clear hierarchy, generous whitespace, consistent alignment.

3. **Warmth through craft** — Warmth comes from typographic care (serif headings, proper number formatting), subtle color (ivory backgrounds, teal accents), and thoughtful transitions — not from illustrations, emojis, or casual copy.

4. **Accessible by default** — Screen reader experience is a first-class concern, not an afterthought. Semantic HTML, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support are non-negotiable. Target WCAG AA with screen reader priority.

5. **Progressive disclosure** — Show the essential answer (net salary) immediately. Let users drill into breakdowns, comparisons, and scenarios at their own pace. Complexity is available but never forced.

### Color Intent
| Role | Token | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| Primary (Sapphire) | `--primary-*` | Trust, links, interactive elements |
| Accent (Teal) | `--accent` | Action, emphasis, the "answer" color |
| Error (Red) | `--error` | Tax burden, warnings, negative deltas |
| Success (Green) | `--success` | Net income, positive indicators |
| Warning (Amber) | `--warning` | Caution, thresholds, regional taxes |
| Neutral (Warm grays) | `--gray-*` | Structure, borders, secondary text |

### Accessibility Commitments
- WCAG AA compliance minimum, with screen reader experience as priority
- All interactive elements keyboard-navigable with visible focus indicators
- Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- No information conveyed by color alone — always paired with text/icons
- Skip links, landmark roles, and live regions for dynamic content
- Italian number formatting (€1.234,56) with proper `aria-label` for screen readers
