# Impeccable Design Context: Quanto Guadagno

## Design Context

### Users

Italian workers who want to know what actually lands in their account. Employees holding a contract offer or a payslip, people with a partita IVA in the forfettario regime, anyone trying to read a cedolino and failing. They arrive with one number, a gross annual figure, and one question. Some stop at the answer. Some want every line that produced it. The page serves both without asking which one they are.

### Brand Personality

Preciso, sobrio, leggibile. Precise, plain, legible.

The site should look like a payslip that was finally typeset by someone who cares. Authority comes from showing the arithmetic, not from decoration. There is no tone of voice beyond stating what the number is and where it came from.

### Emotional Goals

1. Recognition. Someone who has held an Italian payslip should see something familiar in the layout before reading a word.
2. Trust in the figure. Every line of the breakdown names the rule it applies, so the total can be checked instead of believed.
3. Composure. Nothing on the page competes with the number. No badge, no alert colour, no prompt to act.
4. Willingness to read on. People follow the breakdown down the table because the table is easy to read, not because a control promised them more.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual tone:** the Italian busta paga, set properly. Ruled tables, tabular figures, the net amount stated plainly at the end.
- **References:** the cedolino itself, and the Milanese rationalist print tradition around it. Olivetti, Unimark, Vignelli. Black ink on white paper, one spot colour, one grotesk in several widths.
- **Anti-references:** cream paper with serif display type and one italic accent word, which is the editorial template everyone shipped in 2026. Also fintech dashboards, card kits, and any page whose palette alone would let it pass for a generic finance product.
- **Theme:** white paper, black ink. Dark mode is the negative of the same structure, with no warm tint.
- **Typography:** one family, Archivo, in three widths. Expanded 600 for the wordmark and the net figure, regular weights for text, semicondensed for dense table cells on narrow screens. Tabular numerals on every number. No serif, no monospace.

### Design Principles

1. **The number is the content.** The net figure is the largest thing on the page, and everything else is placed relative to it. Type size and spacing exist to get a reader to that figure, then down the table that explains it.

2. **One spot colour.** Blue `#1D3BCB` carries links, focus, the primary action and the rule under the net figure. Nothing else. It should cover less than 5% of any screen. Money is never coloured: a deduction is a negative amount in the trattenute position, set with a real minus sign, not red text.

3. **Rules only where they separate rows.** A 1px line is allowed between table rows and under the header. No cards, no panels, no shadows, no nested borders. Radius is 2px on inputs and buttons, zero everywhere else.

4. **One motion moment.** When an input changes, the net figure settles: 150ms of opacity and a 2px rise. Hover and focus change colour only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the settle does not run.

5. **Sentence case everywhere.** Headings, labels, buttons, navigation. No letterspaced capitals, no eyebrow label above a heading, no arrow appended to a link. A button says what happens when it is pressed.

### Color Intent

| Role | Token | Light | Meaning |
|------|-------|-------|---------|
| Carta | `--carta` | `#FFFFFF` | page and panels |
| Inchiostro | `--inchiostro` | `#000000` | text, input borders, primary button fill |
| Grigio | `--grigio` | `#5C5C5C` | secondary text, 7:1 on white |
| Filetto | `--filetto` | `#D4D4D4` | 1px rules between rows and under the header |
| Velo | `--velo` | `#F3F3F1` | input background, zebra rows, quiet panels |
| Blu | `--blu` | `#1D3BCB` | the one spot ink: links, focus ring, primary action, the rule under the net figure |
| Errore | `--errore` | `#B3261E` | validation text only, always with an "Errore:" prefix |

Dark mode keeps the same roles and inverts the values: carta `#101010`, inchiostro `#F5F5F5`, grigio `#A6A6A6`, filetto `#333333`, velo `#1A1A1A`, blu lifted to `#8FA3FF`.

### Accessibility Commitments

- WCAG AA as the floor, with the screen reader pass treated as part of the design rather than a later fix.
- Black on white and `#5C5C5C` on white both clear AA for body text. Any new pairing is measured before it ships.
- Every interactive element is reachable by keyboard and shows a visible focus ring in blu. No outline is removed without a replacement.
- Errors are announced through a live region and always carry text. Colour never carries meaning on its own, which is part of why amounts are not coloured.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the settle on the net figure. Nothing else animates.
- Amounts use Italian formatting (`€1.234,56`) and are read correctly by a screen reader.
- Skip link, landmark roles, and a results region that announces when the figure changes.
